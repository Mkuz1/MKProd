How it works

Security

  • Your password never leaves your device

    Only a derived key is sent. A second key, which stays with you, unlocks your private key.

  • Authenticator app sign in

    Optional for each person, and the owner can require it for the whole workspace.

  • Keys rotate when someone leaves

    Removing a member creates a new workspace key, so their old copy stops working on new data.

  • Panic mode

    The owner can lock the workspace instantly. Everyone else is signed out until it is unlocked.

In plain words

What the server can still see

We would rather tell you than have you guess. The server knows:

  • Account names, email addresses and workspace names
  • Member roles and whether two factor sign in is on
  • Device labels, network address and last activity
  • Each record's type, size and when it changed

It never sees the titles, notes, amounts, suppliers or files inside them.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first

Who is MK Prod for?

Teams that run projects and purchasing together: operations, production, events and office teams who plan tasks, buy what those tasks need and answer for the budget.

Can MK Prod read my workspace?

No. Tasks, docs, chat, budgets, orders and contracts are encrypted with AES 256 GCM on your device before they sync. The server and the Cloudflare account it runs on only ever store ciphertext.

What can the server still see?

Account names and email addresses, workspace names, member roles, device labels, record sizes and when records change. The content of your work stays encrypted.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the recovery code you saved at sign up. It resets your password and keeps access to all encrypted data. An admin can also reset your password; teammates then share the workspace key with you again.

Which devices does it run on?

A native Mac app for macOS 14 and later, a Windows app that installs without admin rights, an Android app for Android 7 and later including foldables, iPhone and iPad, and any modern browser.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The Mac app keeps a full local copy and the website keeps an encrypted offline cache. Changes sync when you reconnect, and simultaneous edits merge field by field.

How do suppliers respond to an order?

Share an order as a supplier link. The supplier opens it without an account and can confirm, decline or ask a question. Links are encrypted, expire after 7 to 90 days and can be revoked.

Can I bring my data in and take it out?

Yes. Import tasks from CSV and budgets, catalog items and contracts from an Excel template. Export to CSV, Excel or a full JSON backup whenever you like.
