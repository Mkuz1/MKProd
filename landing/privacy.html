Who we are

Your workspace content is encrypted

Tasks, docs, chat, budgets, orders, contracts and file attachments are encrypted in the Mac app or your browser with AES 256 GCM before they are uploaded. The server and the Cloudflare account it runs on only store ciphertext and cannot read it.

What the server can see

  • Account names and email addresses, workspace names and member roles
  • Whether two factor sign in is on for an account
  • Session details: a device label from your browser, network address, approximate city and last activity
  • For each record: its type, a random id, its size, and when and by whom it changed
  • For supplier links: who created them, when they expire, how often they were opened and how many replies they have
  • Which page or task id someone has open, to show presence to teammates

How long we keep it

  • Sign in throttling records, which contain emails and network addresses, are deleted after 24 hours
  • Presence information is deleted after 7 days
  • The security log is kept for one year

Email

We send account emails such as confirmation codes and sign in alerts, and notifications you can switch off per person: assignments, mentions, approvals, contract reminders and the weekly summary.

Your rights

You can export your workspace at any time, delete your account, and as an owner delete or transfer a workspace. Contact {{contact email}} for any other request.
